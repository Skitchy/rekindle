## Assistant
Right. There are a few approaches:

1. **Semaphore-based connection limiting** — cap concurrent connections with a tokio::sync::Semaphore. Simple, but blunt.
2. **Channel-based backpressure** — bounded channel between the acceptor and processor. When the channel is full, the acceptor blocks on send, which naturally stops accepting.
3. **TCP-level backpressure** — stop calling accept() when the processor is overwhelmed. The OS TCP backlog handles the rest.

For your case, I'd go with option 2. The bounded channel gives you a clean separation between accepting and processing, and the backpressure propagates naturally. You also get metrics for free — channel length tells you how close to capacity you are.
